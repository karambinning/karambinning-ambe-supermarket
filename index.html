About the shop

A corner store, plain & useful, in the middle of town.

Ambe Supermarket sits at number 30 on St Andrews Street — the long terrace that runs from the top of Sheep Street out toward Spencer Bridge Road. It is the sort of shop you walk into without a list and leave with what you needed for tonight.

For the full range, fresh stock and what's in this week, come by the shop. We've kept this page short on purpose: a small, honest record that the shop is here, what it's called, and where to find it.

Come in. You'll know it when you see it.
